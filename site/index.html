Features

  • Always today

    It shows the real day and turns over at midnight by itself, so it's right even if it runs for weeks. Tap any day to preview it.

  • Breathing letters

    Each letter stretches and settles on its own slow rhythm, so the word never moves the same way twice. Tap the word to pause it.

  • Seven languages

    English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Persian and Hebrew, each in a typeface made for its script, with right-to-left handled properly.

  • Your look

    Any background and text colour, five display fonts from clean to retro, and a dimmer for a softer screen at night.

  • Any screen

    The word sizes itself to fit, from a phone to a 4K monitor, and stays sharp on Retina and high-resolution displays.

  • Offline and private

    One file with the fonts built in. No accounts, no tracking and no network requests. Your settings stay in your browser.

Use it as your wallpaper

https://shazmoghaddam.github.io/animated-weekdays-live-wallpaper/wallpaper/

  1. Mac

    Install Plash, free from the Mac App Store. Choose Add Website from its menu bar icon and paste the link above. The wallpaper sits behind your windows and icons.

  2. Windows

    Install Lively Wallpaper, free from the Microsoft Store. Press the plus button, paste the link above and press the arrow. It runs live on your desktop.

  3. Any screen

    Open the wallpaper in any browser and press the full screen button in the bottom right. Good for a second monitor, a tablet or a TV.
